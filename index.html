Software-controlled electrical fault training

A training system that lets an instructor assign hidden faults to a physical electrical board, then observe learners diagnose the equipment.

The interesting part is not the board alone. It is the complete interaction between the instructor application, networked controller, physical fault switching and the learner’s test process.

Problem
Create realistic, repeatable fault-finding practice without revealing the fault.
My contribution
Concept, system design, software, electronics and training workflow.

PLC plant simulators with consequences

Interactive plant simulations driven by a learner’s real PLC program—not by hidden application logic.

The simulators expose weak control logic through believable results: contactor conflicts, overloads, dry running and failed interlocks. Incorrect programs remain visible instead of being silently corrected.

Scenarios
Roller door, duplex pump station, cold room and access control.
Design decision
The PLC owns the sequence; the simulator owns the physical response.
