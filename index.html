Training system

Electrical fault-training boards

I developed a system that allows an instructor to place hidden faults on a physical electrical training board. Learners then use normal test equipment and fault-finding methods to diagnose the problem.

The project combines a teacher application, Raspberry Pi controllers, network communication, electronic fault switching and the physical training equipment.

PLC training

Industrial plant simulators

I built browser-based simulations that connect to a learner’s PLC. The PLC controls the plant, while the simulator provides sensors, operator controls and realistic equipment behaviour.

Incorrect PLC logic remains visible. For example, poor control can cause an overload, conflicting contactors or a pump running dry.

JavaScript · Python · GPIO · Modbus TCP
