I combine software knowledge with a practical understanding of hardware, electronics and control systems. I use AI to build and refine solutions faster, while applying my own technical judgement to identify what will work in practice.

I work with people to understand how their work actually gets done and where software or automation can make a difference. This lets me find opportunities others miss.

By automating repetitive tasks and connecting systems that were not designed to work together, I create efficiencies and new capabilities that were not previously thought possible.
