Capability

Technical teaching and training systems

Technical teaching supported by purpose-built systems for practising circuit analysis, PLC control, electrical testing and fault-finding. The PLC Simulator connects physical control hardware to an expandable range of plant scenarios.

Fault Testing Boards

A physical training platform used by apprentices to complete mandatory electrical testing in a safe, simulated environment. The powered display records correct tests, incorrect attempts and missed faults.

Fault-board control hardware

The rear assembly combines a Raspberry Pi, relay banks, power conversion and field wiring to introduce controlled faults and evaluate testing sequences.

SΩLVE IT!

A gamified training tool that teaches apprentice electricians to recognise and simplify single-path and multipath circuits.

Drone technology and engineering electronics

Specialist technical teaching connecting component-level electronics with drone systems, operation and practical applications.

PLC Simulator — cold room

One of an expandable range of scenarios controlled by real industrial PLC hardware, allowing learners to test sequences, interlocks and fault responses without live machinery.

PLC Simulator — pump station

Training in float-based level control, alarms, protection, and duty/duty or duty/standby pump configurations using physical PLC hardware.

PLC Simulator — roller door

A roller-door scenario covering pushbutton control, travel limits, emergency stop, contactor interlocking and fault response using physical PLC hardware.
