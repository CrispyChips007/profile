Capability

Training systems and simulations

Physical and simulated systems built for practising PLC control, electrical testing and fault-finding without relying on live plant.

Fault Testing Boards

A physical training platform used by apprentices to complete mandatory electrical testing in a safe, simulated environment. The powered display records correct tests, incorrect attempts and missed faults.

Fault-board control hardware

The rear assembly combines a Raspberry Pi, relay banks, power conversion and field wiring to introduce controlled faults and evaluate testing sequences.

SaaS training platform

A software-led training environment for guided fault finding, testing practice and repeatable assessment activities.

PLC Simulator

A real industrial PLC controls a simulated cold-room plant, allowing learners to test sequences, interlocks and fault responses without live machinery.

Two-pump pump station

A duplex pump-station simulation for practising level control, duty and assist operation, protection and alarm handling.

Industrial roller door

A roller-door simulation covering pushbutton control, travel limits, emergency stop, contactor interlocking and fault response.
